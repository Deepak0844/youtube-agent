- sagitta: (r: number, d: number) => number - * How far a sphere of radius r "curves away" from the straight line tangent to it, over an * arc of length d. This is the sagitta, r·(1 − cos(d/r)). * * NOTE, because it is the whole video and it is easy to get wrong: for something in orbit at * 400 km, "the Earth curving away beneath it" is the curvature of the SHELL IT IS ON * (r = R + h), not of the ground (r = R). The ground, being more sharply curved, drops 4.62 m * over a 7.67 km chord — but the distance from the station to the ground is what must stay * constant, and that is governed by r = R + h, which drops 4.35 m. That 4.35 equals the 4.35 m * the station falls in the same second — necessarily, because that equality IS what a circular * orbit is. We show the shell number, because the shell number is the one that is exactly true.
